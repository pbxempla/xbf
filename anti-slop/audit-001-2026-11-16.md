# Anti-slop audit 001

**Scope:** Mapping, upload, dashboard, and their shared upload components.  
**Mode:** After. This is an audit only; no product UI files were changed.

## Findings

1. **HIGH - Keyboard access for file selection (R-32, R-39).**  
   [FileDropzone.tsx](../components/upload/FileDropzone.tsx#L53) makes a non-focusable `div` clickable to open the file picker. The nested Browse button works by keyboard, but the larger drop area does not. Use a native, keyboard-operable control for the full selection area and retain drag-and-drop as an enhancement.

2. **HIGH - Insufficient contrast for muted text (R-25).**  
   [UploadTitleSection.tsx](../components/upload/UploadTitleSection.tsx#L25), [FileDropzone.tsx](../components/upload/FileDropzone.tsx#L106), and [DataPreviewTable.tsx](../components/upload/DataPreviewTable.tsx#L110) use `#8d969e` for text. The measured contrast against white is **3.00:1**, below 4.5:1 for normal text. Replace these text uses with a darker token and verify each background pairing.

3. **HIGH - Mapping needs a phone-sized layout (R-03).**  
   [page.tsx](../app/mapping/page.tsx#L431) fixes the mapping table at a minimum width of 760px and puts it in a horizontal scroller. On a phone, users must pan sideways to read and operate the field selectors. Reflow each source column and its target selector into a stacked mobile row.

4. **HIGH - Design direction needs a decision before rebuilding (R-37).**  
   [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md#L588) defines cobalt violet as the sole brand stamp, while the design-taste skill prohibits violet in the page palette. It also says logged-in app surfaces are out of scope ([DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md#L634)). The mapping page currently uses a lavender gradient and indigo controls ([page.tsx](../app/mapping/page.tsx#L321)). Choose whether to keep Xempla violet as an explicit override or drop violet for this app surface; the existing marketing direction does not settle that conflict.

5. **LOW - Mapping copy and repeated metadata distract from the task (R-05, C-3).**  
   [page.tsx](../app/mapping/page.tsx#L343) includes a stage label, schema version, delimiter, vendor/model terminology, decision-layer status, and a long explanation of matching internals. The source-column chips at [page.tsx](../app/mapping/page.tsx#L554) repeat information already shown in the table. [dashboard/page.tsx](../app/dashboard/page.tsx#L106) also adds a generic overview label and welcome sentence before the upload action. Reduce this to the mapping action, required-field guidance, concise statuses, and a short privacy disclosure that preserves what data is sent.

6. **LOW - Mapping preparation errors have no recovery action (R-27, C-4).**  
   [page.tsx](../app/mapping/page.tsx#L132) stores a `prepareMapping` failure in `mappingError`, but the error state offers no retry. The Jev retry control only appears after a proposal exists. Add a clear retry action for proposal preparation failures.

## Direction for the approved fixes

`DESIGN.md` is the available brand reference, but it explicitly excludes logged-in app surfaces. The existing upload and dashboard screens use a black header and neutral surfaces, while mapping introduces a lavender gradient and indigo controls. Once the palette decision in finding 4 is made, align the mapping and connected upload flow to one restrained application treatment. Keep user-facing copy limited to choices and guidance needed to complete the import.

The approved fixes and their validation are recorded in the [follow-up report](./audit-001-followup-2026-11-16.md).
